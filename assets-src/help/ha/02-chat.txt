Allon hira da kowane maɓalli
## saman allon
- Menu: yana buɗe aljihunan hagu tare da hirarku.
- Taɗi / Terminal / Code / Preview: canza babban ra'ayi. Hira ita ce zance; Terminal shine ainihin tashar; Lambar ita ce filin aiki na fayil tare da Git; Preview shine ginannen mai bincike.
- Aura: yana nuna allon hali mai rai. Maɓallan gefensa sune Mic, Chat (komawa ga tattaunawar) da Refresh (sake haɗa gajimare kuma yana sake loda lissafin ƙirar).
- Saituna: yana buɗe sassan saituna takwas.
- Banner yana nuna yanayin makirufo: "Saurara..."", "Mic off — tap", ko "Magana… matsa don tsayawa".
- Lokacin da sabon sigar ya shirya wani banner na ɗaukaka yana bayyana tare da Zazzagewa, Shigarwa da Ba da izini maɓallan (duba Sabuntawa).

## Menu na hagu (tattaunawa)
- "+ Sabuwar hira": fara sabon tattaunawa.
- Kowace taɗi a cikin jeri yana nuna alamar gudu yayin da wakilin ke aiki a wurin. fensir ya sake suna; giciye yana goge shi (bayan tabbatarwa: an goge duk saƙonnin da ke cikinsa).
- Jigo: zaɓi Horror (jini, wuta da inuwa), Duhu (glassin duhu mai tsafta) ko Haske (gilashin haske).

## Bar sakon
- Haɗa (takarda): Hoton gallery, Hoton kyamara, Binciken daftarin aiki (yana juya rubutun a cikin hoto zuwa .docx mai gyara), raba allon don wakili ya gan shi ("Kallon allo" a kunne ko kashe), da fayilolin mai jiwuwa ko bidiyo. Abubuwan da aka makala suna nunawa azaman guntu; giciye yana cire daya, "Clear all" yana kawar da duka.
- Maɓallin yanayi: Wakili ko Gina.
- Maɓallin mic: yana kunna ko kashe makirufo mai saurare.
- Maɓallin ƙira: yana buɗe lissafin ƙirar tare da akwatin nema. Samfuran da aka yiwa alama "Live" suna iya magana da muryar su.
- Aika: aika sako. Yayin da wakilin ke aiki wannan ya zama Tsayawa.
- Shigarwar ta ce "Me zan yi?" a Yanayin Agent da "Me zan gina? Faɗa shi gajere" a cikin Yanayin Gina.

## Saƙonni
- Kwafi, Raba da Ji (yana karanta saƙon da ƙarfi; sake danna don tsayawa) suna ƙarƙashin kowane saƙo.
- "Tunani" yana nuna dalilin samfurin lokacin da samfurin ya ba da shi.
- Lambar da wakilin ya rubuta yana bayyana kai tsaye a cikin akwati, layi ta layi, tare da Kwafi, Zazzagewa da Buɗe.
- Fayilolin da wakilin ke nunawa azaman katunan tare da Buɗe (a cikin ainihin ƙa'idar wayar), Kunna, Raba da "Sauran app…".
- Hotuna, sauti da bidiyo suna nuna adadin ci gaba kai tsaye yayin da ake yin su.
- Katunan kayan aiki suna nuna abin da wakili ke yi: Gudu, aikata, nasara ko kasawa.

## Panel (todo, kayan aiki, fayiloli, ƙananan wakilai)
- Todo board: shirin da wakilin ya rubuta don babban aiki da ci gabansa (n/ jimlar aikata). Kuna iya ƙara aikin ku kuma share ayyuka.
- Kayan aiki: jerin kowane kiran kayan aiki, tare da matsayi mai rai.
- Fayiloli: kowane fayil da wakilin ya ƙirƙira, tare da Buɗe, Raba, Bugawa da Share (tare da tabbatarwa) da akwatin bincike.
- Sub-wakilai: wakilai masu taimako suna gudana a bango, tare da maɓallin Tsaya.
- Tasha tab: ainihin tashar tashar (duba Fayilolin Fayiloli da Babin Tasha).

## Kwamitin samfoti
- Baya, Gaba, Tsayawa, Sake lodi da sandar adireshin (buga adireshin gidan yanar gizo, ko bincika Google).
- Buɗe fayil: yana nuna Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, hotuna, Markdown, CSV, lamba da rukunoni a cikin app.
- Girman: raguwa ko ƙara girman panel.
- Gyara: yana aika matsalolin shafin (kurakurai na na'ura, gazawar lodi) zuwa wakili, wanda ke gyara su a bango.
- Zaɓi sashi: matsa wani ɓangaren shafin, sannan gaya wa wakilin abin da zai yi da wannan ɓangaren.
- Maɓallin wakili: tambayi wani abu game da shafin; wakilin ya karbi hotonsa.
- Buɗewa da sauri: gajerun hanyoyi zuwa shafuka na baya-bayan nan.

## Wurin aiki Code
- Hagu: fayilolinku (sabon fayil, buɗe, adana).
- Edita: yana nuna layi da haruffa; canje-canjen da wakili ya yi ya bayyana a lokaci ɗaya.
- Git: sabon repo, clone daga GitHub, duba, matsayi, aikata tare da saƙo, tura, ja, rassan, alama.
- Ja buƙatun: ƙirƙira, haɗa, rufe. Ayyukan aiki: lissafin yana gudana, gudanar da aikin aiki. Log yana nuna fitarwar umarni.
