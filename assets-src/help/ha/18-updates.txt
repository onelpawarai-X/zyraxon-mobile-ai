Sabuntawa
Saituna → Sabuntawa → Sabunta aikace-aikacen, ko banner a saman ƙa'idar.
- Ka'idar tana duba sabbin abubuwan da aka fitar kuma tana nuna "Sabuwar sabuntawa akwai - sigar".
- Bincika don sabuntawa: tambaya yanzu. Idan kuna halin yanzu yana cewa haka.
- Zazzagewa & shigar: abubuwan zazzagewa tare da kaso. Idan an gama danna Shigar. Hirarku, ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya da saitunanku suna tsayawa.
- Idan Android ya toshe shigarwa, danna maɓallin izini, ba da izini ZYRAXON a matsayin tushen shigarwa, sannan sake gwadawa.
- Maɓallan banner: Zazzagewa, Shigarwa, Ba da izini, da Rufe.
