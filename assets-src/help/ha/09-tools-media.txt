Hotuna, sauti, bidiyo, takardu da ƙira
- `generate_image` (cibiyar sadarwa): yi hoto daga bayanin (rubuta shi da daki-daki; Turanci yana aiki mafi kyau). Yana bayyana a cikin taɗi tare da maɓallin raba. Don da yawa, nemi da yawa.
- `generate_audio` (cibiyar sadarwa): sautin magana (.mp3) daga kowane rubutu. Don tattaunawa ko labari, ba kowane bangare muryarsa (jinsi, shekaru, harshe, sautin). Harsuna da yawa, gami da Bengali, Hindi da Ingilishi.
- `generate_video`: ainihin bidiyo da aka gyara (ba fim ɗin AI ba) daga fage - hotuna, shirye-shiryen bidiyo, rubutu, launi, labari, taken magana, kiɗa. `ffmpeg`: duk wani gyara kai tsaye - yanke, haɗawa, mai rufi, juzu'i, saurin, haɗin sauti, juyawa. Bidiyo na mintuna 5-10 yana yiwuwa, an gina mataki-mataki.
- Ci gaba yana nunawa kai tsaye a matsayin kashi a cikin hira; idan an gama fayil ɗin ya zo tare da maɓallin Play da Share.
- `make_document`: ainihin Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) ko PowerPoint (.pptx) fayil akan wayar, babu buƙatar shigarwa, kowane harshe. `office_template`: shirye-shiryen ofis tare da guraben cikawa (bar aikace-aikacen, aikace-aikacen aiki, daftari, CV, shawarwarin kasuwanci, zance, sanarwa, mintuna ganawa, kwangila, asusun wata-wata da ƙari).
- Takardu daga hoto: haɗa hoto (Doc scan). Ana kwafin rubutun daidai kamar yadda aka rubuta (ana yiwa kalmomin da ba a bayyana ba), tebur ya zama .xlsx, in ba haka ba .docx.
- Bayanan taro: daga sauti, kwafi ko abin da kuke faɗa. Takardun yana da taƙaice, yanke shawara, abubuwan aiki (aiki, mai shi, ranar ƙarshe) da kuma shirye-shiryen imel.
- `make_design`: fastoci, tutoci, tambura, katunan, labarai, masu rufe kamar PNG da PDF. `brand_kit`: ba da tambari ko launuka; an ajiye su, jigon ƙa'idar ya canza, kuma daga nan a kan takardu, nunin faifai da shafuka suna amfani da launukan alamar ku.
- `present_slides`: Halin yana magana yayin da katunan nunin nuni; nunin faifai suna canzawa da kansu lokacin da muryar ta ƙare. Nemi fayil ɗin .pptx idan kuna son fayil.
- `open_file`: yana buɗe abin da aka yi a ainihin manhajar wayar (Word/Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDF, gallery, browser) ko a cikin Preview panel; wakilin yana duba allon don tabbatar da ya buɗe.
