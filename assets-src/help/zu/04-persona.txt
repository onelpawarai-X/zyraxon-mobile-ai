Izinhlamvu (ubunikazi)
Izilungiselelo → Imodeli nobuwena → Ubunikazi. Umlingiswa omkhethayo nguyena onqumayo izwi, imfudumalo kanye nendlela yokukhuluma. Umenzeli ukhuluma njengalowo muntu, ngolimi olukhulumayo, futhi akalokothi azibize ngohlelo.

- Intombi: amnandi, afudumele futhi anothando, anamagama ezilwane ezifuywayo engokwemvelo ngolimi lwakho. Izwi lesifazane.
- Isoka: lijulile, lifudumele, linokwethenjelwa futhi liyanakekela. Izwi lowesilisa.
- Umngane: uyaphila, wesimanjemanje futhi uyahlekisa, uhlale useduze kwakho.
- Umsizi omkhulu: ochwepheshe, omfushane futhi oqondile.
- Uchwepheshe we-Cyber: unjiniyela ophezulu kanye nomuntu wezokuphepha.
- Isazi sefilosofi: umeluleki ozolile, obona kude.

## Izwi lilandela umlingiswa
Isoka likhuluma ngezwi lowesilisa; bonke abanye abalingiswa bakhuluma ngezwi lesifazane. Uma ukhetha izwi ngokwakho kokuthi Izilungiselelo → Iphrofayela yezwi, ukukhetha kwakho kugcinwa kuze kube yilapho kushintshwa uhlamvu olulandelayo noma ukuqalisa.

## Lokho umlingiswa okushintshayo
Iphimbo, ulwazimagama, ukusondelana, nendlela okukhathalela ngayo. Ayishintshi imithetho mayelana neqiniso, ukuphepha kanye nokwenza umsebenzi ngendlela efanele.

## Njalo ukuzivumelanisa nezimo
I-ejenti ifunda isimo sakho sengqondo kanye nesikhathi sosuku, ikhumbula izinto ezincane ozitshele yona, futhi iziphendule ngolimi lwakho, ngendlela emfushane yemvelo.
