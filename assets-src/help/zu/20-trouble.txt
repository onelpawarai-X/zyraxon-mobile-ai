Ukuxazulula inkinga
- Ayikho impendulo: hlola i-inthanethi yakho kanye nokhiye we-API (Izilungiselelo → Abahlinzeki ne-API ibonisa iphutha langempela). Zama enye imodeli noma imodeli ekudivayisi.
- Imakrofoni ayenzi lutho: vumela imvume yemakrofoni; hlola "Gcina imakrofoni ivuliwe"; hlola ulimi lokuqashelwa.
- ZYRAXON izizwa ngokwayo: lokhu kuhlunga ngombhalo; uma kwenzeka, yehlisa ivolumu noma usebenzise ama-headphone.
- Umusho uvela kabili: fixed ezinguqulweni zamanje; buyekeza uhlelo lokusebenza.
- Ngeke ithephe noma ifunde isikrini: vula isevisi yokufinyeleleka (Izilungiselelo → Izimvume).
- Ima ngemuva: vumela izaziso nokukhululwa kokuthuthukisa ibhethri; imbondela yeso elintantayo.
- Ithuluzi lilokhu licela imvume: shintsha ileveli kokuthi Izilungiselelo → Okuzenzakalelayo, noma phendula ikhadi.
- Play Protect ivimba okuthile: bona isahluko Sezimvume, noma vumela umenzeli aqondise izinyathelo.
- Itheminali noma i-ZYRAXON OS ayiqali: linda ukukhishwa kokuqala ekuqaleni kohlelo lokusebenza; hlola isitoreji samahhala.
- Isibuyekezo ngeke sifake: vumela i-ZYRAXON njengomthombo wokufaka.
- Ayikwazi ukushicilela: engeza GitHub ithokheni ye-GitHub; ukuze uthole isixhumanisi somphakathi umenzeli angasebenzisa umhubhe.
- Isahluko sikhombisa isiNgisi: leso sahluko asinawo umbhalo ngolimi lwakho kulesi sakhiwo; buyekeza uhlelo lokusebenza.
- Okuthile okungajwayelekile: sebenzisa Izilungiselelo → Ukuzulazula → Iseshini entsha ukuze uqale ukuhlanza (isula umlando wengxoxo kuphela; ukuhlala kwenkumbulo).
