Zonke izilungiselelo zesikrini, ngasinye ngasinye
Izilungiselelo zinezigaba eziyisishiyagalombili. Ingxenye ngayinye inamaphilisi ezikrinini zayo ezingaphansi. Isigaba sokugcina osisebenzisile sivulwa kabusha ngokuzayo.

## 1. Ulimi nokuhumusha → Uhlelo lokusebenza nolimi lwethuluzi
Khetha ulimi lwalo lonke uhlelo lokusebenza kanye nemilayezo yethuluzi lomenzeli. Sesha uhlu bese uthinta okukodwa. Isikrini sibonisa ulimi lwamanje nokuthi ukuhumusha kwe-UI nokuhumusha komlayezo wethuluzi kuvuliwe. Izilimi ezingu-60 ziyatholakala. Bheka isahluko esithi Ulimi.

## 2. Izwi & imakrofoni
- Iphrofayili yezwi: khetha izwi lowesifazane noma lowesilisa, isivinini, iphimbo, "Funda izimpendulo ngokuzwakalayo", Izwi lokuhlola, iseluleko sokulanda izwi lesiBengali.
- Imakrofoni nolimi: ulimi lokuqaphela, gcina imakrofoni ivuliwe, thumela ngokuzenzakalelayo uma uyeka ukukhuluma.
- Izwi elibukhoma: ukukhuluma-kuya-inkulumo kuvuliwe/kuvaliwe, isimo, uhlu lwamamodeli esikhathi sangempela.

## 3. Abahlinzeki & I-API
- Abahlinzeki be-AI: okhiye bakho be-API (ZYRAXON, OpenCode isabelo samahhala, Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Claude, DeepSeek, OpenRouter] kanye ZYRAXON Apex ne-ejenti yamafu. Ukhiye olondoloziwe uqinisekiswa ngokufunda ikhathalogi yangempela yomhlinzeki futhi ubonisa inani lamamodeli noma iphutha eliqondile. Inkinobho ivula iwebhusayithi ye-ZYRAXON lapho udala khona ukhiye.
- GitHub: ithokheni yakho GitHub yokufinyelela (idinga ububanzi be-repo) ukuze ishicilelwe, Git izicelo zokusunduza nokudonsa. Londoloza ithokheni noma uyisuse; isimo sikhombisa uma sisethiwe.

## 4. Izibuyekezo → Isibuyekezo sohlelo lokusebenza
Ibonisa inguqulo yamanje, ihlolela ukukhishwa okusha, iyilande ngebha yokuqhubeka bese iyayifaka. Izingxoxo zakho, inkumbulo nezilungiselelo kuhlala. Uma i-Android idinga imvume yokufaka, inkinobho ivula leso silungiselelo.

## 5. Imodeli nobuwena
- Ubunikazi: khetha umlingiswa (bheka Isahluko Sabalingisi).
- Imodeli: ikhathalogi yemodeli ebukhoma enebhokisi lokusesha, "Wonke amamodeli" nezihlungi "Zezifiso"; amamodeli asheshayo kuqala.
- Kudivayisi: landa, layisha futhi khipha amamodeli asebenza ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi; ihlungwe ezincane kuya ezinkulu; ithokheni Hugging Face ozikhethela yona; ibonisa izifunda ezilayishiwe, ezilayishwayo nezihlulekile.

## 6. I-Aura netimu
- Itimu: Okuthusayo, Okumnyama noma Ukukhanya.
- I-Aura & isizinda: isitayela se-aura nombala (igazi, pink, violet, isibhakabhaka, esiluhlaza, umlilo) ngokulawula umbala omuhle; usayizi we-aura, amandla okukhanya, isivinini sokujikeleza, ukukhanya kwephaneli; iso elintantayo (livuliwe/livaliwe, idizayini, unqenqema lwesikrini, imiyalo yezwi/amagama okuvusa, engeza owakho); imodi ye-shader; i-URL yangemuva yangokwezifiso (i-mp4, i-gif noma isithombe); isivinini sokugqwayiza.

## 7. Ukuphepha nobumfihlo
- Ukhiye wohlelo lokusebenza: iphasiwedi (amadijithi angu-4–8) noma iphethini yamachashazi angu-3×3; qinisekisa ngokungena kabili; khipha ilokhi.
- Izimvume: uhlu olusesahluko sezimvume, ngosizo lwe-Play Protect.
- Ubumfihlo nedatha: yonke idatha ihlala ocingweni. Izinkinobho: susa yonke inkumbulo, susa umlando wengxoxo, susa GitHub ithokheni.

## 8. Uhlelo
Lesi sigaba sinomugqa waso wezinkinobho eziskrola emaceleni, ngakho-ke akukho okucindezelwayo.
- Okuzenzakalelayo: ileveli yokugunyaza kanye nedivayisi kuphela (bona amaleveli okugunyaza).
- Ukuzulazula: uhlu lwezindawo ongagxumela kuzo — "azikho izinkinobho phezulu, yonke into isilapha" — okuhlanganisa igobolondo lesistimu ye-ZX, inkinobho ethi "Iseshini entsha" (isula umlando wengxoxo ukuze iqale kahle), inani lomlayezo kanye nokuguqula imakrofoni.
- Inkumbulo: inkumbulo yendawo SQL enamaqiniso ZYRAXON iyakhumbula; engeza ukhiye kanye nenani; susa konke.
- Umhlahlandlela wamakhono: uhlu olusheshayo lwamakhono ayisibonelo (izinhlelo zokusebenza zemiyalezo, isakhiwo sewebhusayithi, isifundi sesikrini, ithoshi, ikhamera, Wi-Fi nezilungiselelo) enemisho eyisibonelo.
- Ihabhu le-automation: amakhono, ukushayela okuzenzakalelayo, imali, umugqa wesikhathi, ingqondo ne-ejenti yokushaya ucingo endaweni eyodwa (bona ihabhu le-Automation).

## 9. Imibhalo
Isigaba sayo kokuthi Izilungiselelo, esihlukene neSistimu. Lo mhlahlandlela: izahluko ezingamashumi amabili nanye, ibhokisi lokusesha, kanye nomugqa we-chip wolimi. Lonke ulimi kuhlelo lokusebenza selubhalwe ngokugcwele ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza — akukho okuhunyushwayo ocingweni lwakho futhi akukho inthanethi edingekayo ukuze uyifunde.
