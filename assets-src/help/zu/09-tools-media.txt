Izithombe, umsindo, ividiyo, imibhalo kanye nomklamo
- `generate_image` (inethiwekhi): yenza isithombe ngencazelo (sibhale ngemininingwane; isiNgisi sisebenza kahle kakhulu). Ivela engxoxweni enezinkinobho zokwabelana. Kwabaningi, cela eziningana.
- `generate_audio` (inethiwekhi): umsindo okhulunywayo (.mp3) ovela kunoma yimuphi umbhalo. Ngengxoxo noma indaba, nika ingxenye ngayinye izwi layo (ubulili, ubudala, ulimi, iphimbo). Izilimi eziningi, okuhlanganisa isiBengali, isiHindi nesiNgisi.
- `generate_video`: ividiyo ehleliwe yangempela (hhayi ividiyo ye-AI) evela ezigcawini — izithombe, iziqeshana, umbhalo, umbala, ukulandisa, amazwibela, umculo. `ffmpeg`: noma yikuphi ukuhlela okuqondile — sika, hlanganisa, imbondela, imibhalo engezansi, isivinini, imiksi yomsindo, guqula. Amavidiyo amaminithi angu-5–10 angenzeka, akhiwe isinyathelo ngesinyathelo.
- Inqubekelaphambili ibonisa bukhoma njengephesenti engxoxweni; uma seliqedile ifayela lifika nezinkinobho Dlala futhi Yabelana.
- `make_document`: ifayela langempela Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) noma PowerPoint (.pptx) ifayela ocingweni, akukho ukufakwa okudingekayo, noma yiluphi ulimi. `office_template`: amaphepha asehhovisi aselungile anezikhala okumele zigcwaliswe (shiya isicelo, isicelo somsebenzi, i-invoyisi, i-CV, isiphakamiso sebhizinisi, ikhotheshini, isaziso, amaminithi omhlangano, inkontileka, ama-akhawunti anyanga zonke nokunye).
- Idokhumenti evela esithombeni: namathisela isithombe (Iskeni seDokhumenti). Umbhalo ukopishwe njengoba ubhaliwe (amagama angacacile amakiwe), ithebula liba .xlsx, ngaphandle kwalokho .docx.
- Amanothi omhlangano: kusuka kumsindo, okulotshiweyo noma lokho okushoyo. Idokhumenti inesifinyezo, izinqumo, izinto zesenzo (umsebenzi, umnikazi, idethi yamanqamu) kanye nokusalungiswa kwe-imeyili esilungile.
- `make_design`: amaphosta, izibhengezo, amalogo, amakhadi, izindaba, amakhava njenge-PNG kanye ne-PDF. `brand_kit`: nikeza ilogo noma imibala; agciniwe, itimu yohlelo lokusebenza iyashintsha, futhi kusukela lapho kuqhubeke amadokhumenti, amaslayidi namasayithi asebenzisa imibala yomkhiqizo wakho.
- `present_slides`: umlingisi uyakhuluma ngenkathi amakhadi ama-slide ekhombisa; amaslayidi azishintsha ngokwawo lapho izwi liqeda. Cela ifayela le-.pptx uma ufuna ifayela.
- `open_file`: ivula lokho okwenziwe ohlelweni lokusebenza lwangempela lwefoni (Word/Amadokhumenti, AmaSpredishithi, Amaslayidi, PDF, igalari, isiphequluli) noma kuphaneli yokubuka kuqala; umenzeli uhlola isikrini ukuze aqiniseke ukuthi sivuliwe.
