Ihabhu le-automation
Izilungiselelo → Isistimu → Ihabhu lokuzenzakalela. Inkinobho ngayinye yalokho ZYRAXON engakwenza ngokwayo isendaweni eyodwa, kumathebhu ayisithupha. Ithebhu ngayinye iqala ngencazelo emfushane yokuthi isebenza kanjani.

## Amakhono
Fundisa ZYRAXON umsebenzi ngezindlela ezimbili: yitshele engxoxweni ("funda lo msebenzi"), noma cindezela inkinobho yokurekhoda bese uwenza ngokwakho umsebenzi kolunye uhlelo lokusebenza, bese uyabuya bese ucindezela Misa. Isevisi yokufinyeleleka kufanele ivulwe, noma ompompi bakho bangabonakali. Okufundile kuvela ngaphansi kokuthi "Amakhono Afundiwe" futhi kungadlalwa futhi noma kususwe.

## I-Autopilot
Imithetho yohlobo "uma lokhu kwenzeka, yenza lokho". Uma umcimbi wenzeka ZYRAXON uzivusa yedwa enze umsebenzi ngaphandle kokubuza. Ungakwazi futhi ukudala umthetho ngokuwusho engxoxweni. Umthetho ngamunye ukhombisa ukuthi ususebenze kangaki.

## Imali
Ifunda i-SMS evela kunoma yiliphi ibhange noma isikhwama semali kunoma yiliphi izwe (PayPal, M-Pesa, UPI, Pix, bKash, amakhadi…) futhi igcina ileja eyodwa yemali engenayo, ukusetshenziswa kwemali, izinkokhelo nebhalansi. Ungasetha ibhajethi futhi uthole isexwayiso lapho usidlulisa. Isebenza ocingweni, futhi akukho okuthunyelwa noma kuphi.

## Umugqa wesikhathi
Konke ZYRAXON akwenzile kubhalwe lapha. Ukubhalwa kwefayela, ukuhlelwa nokususwa kungahlehliswa ngenkinobho eyodwa. Umyalo oqalisiwe awukwazi ukuhlehliswa.

## Ubuchopho
Isesha amafayela akho (umbhalo, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV) futhi, uma uyivumela, i-SMS yakho, ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nanganoma yiluphi ulimi. Cindezela "Inkomba" kuqala, bese usesha.

## Shayela umenzeli
Uma ungakwazi ukuthatha ucingo, u-ZYRAXON uyaphendula, ukhuluma ngolimi lwalowo ofonayo, ufunda igama nesizathu, futhi ikunikeza isifinyezo lapho ucingo luphela. Izilimi eziyishumi nanhlanu zisebenzisa imithetho eyakhelwe ngaphakathi ngokushesha; zonke ezinye izilimi ziphathwa yimodeli, ephendula ngolimi lwalowo ofonayo. Amagama asho ukuvalelisa, ukuphuthuma noma ugaxekile aqondwa kuzo zonke izilimi ngesikhathi esisodwa. Izifinyezo zezingcingo ezibanjiwe zikleliswe kuthebhu.
