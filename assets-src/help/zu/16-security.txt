Ukuphepha kanye nobumfihlo
- Yonke idatha ka-ZYRAXON iphila kufoni yakho: izingxoxo, inkumbulo, ileja, amakhono, umugqa wesikhathi, inkomba yobuchopho. Akukho okugcinwe kuseva ye-ZYRAXON.
- Amamodeli wamafu athola kuphela lokho okudingekayo ngesikhathi osithumelayo. Ngokuthi "Kudivayisi kuphela" akukho lutho oluthunyelwayo.
- Ukwaziswa namafayela olimi athunyelwe kuhlelo lokusebenza abethelwe ngaphakathi kokuthi APK.
- Ukukhiya uhlelo lokusebenza: setha iphasiwedi noma iphethini kokuthi Izilungiselelo → Ukuphepha nobumfihlo → Ukhiye wohlelo lokusebenza. ZYRAXON bese uyakhiya uma uyivula noma ubuya ngemuva. "Khipha ukukhiya" kususa iphasiwedi elondoloziwe noma iphethini.
- Amaleveli okugunyaza aqapha lonke ucingo lwamathuluzi ngekhodi (bona amazinga okugunyaza). Amathuluzi angaziwa ahlala ebuza.
- Ungasula: yonke inkumbulo, umlando wengxoxo, nethokheni yakho ye-GitHub, ngayinye enenkinobho eyodwa kokuthi Ubumfihlo Nedatha.
- Ukuphepha: uma uthi othile uyakusongela noma uyakulandela, noma ufuna ifoni noma idatha yakho, i-ejenti ivala ifoni kuqala (uma imvume yomphathi wedivaysi ivuliwe) bese ikhuluma nawe ngomoya ophansi.
- Izinkokhelo: ngaphambi kwanoma iyiphi inkokhelo noma ukudlulisa i-ejenti isho ukuthi izokhokha ini futhi ikucele ukuthi uqinisekise.
- Ukwethembeka: i-ejenti ithi kuphela into esebenzile ngemva kokubona umphumela. Ayilokothi isungule izixhumanisi, amafayela noma imiphumela.
- Isivikelo Sokuqukethwe: noma yini umenzeli eyifunda ngaphandle — isikrini solunye uhlelo lokusebenza, ikhasi lewebhu, umlayezo, ifayela, iseva exhunyiwe — ihlolwa ngekhodi ukuze kubhalwe umbhalo ozama ukunikeza ama-oda e-ejenti (ngezilimi eziningi, okuhlanganisa umbhalo ofihliwe onezinhlamvu ezingabonakali). Umbhalo osolisayo ubiyelwe ngakho-ke imodeli iwuthatha njengedatha kuphela. Yeka ukuthi kuqine kangakanani kulandela izinga lakho lokugunyaza: Ikhiyiwe iphatha noma yini esolisayo njengenobutha; Ucingo olugadiwe umbhalo osolisayo futhi, lapho umbhalo unobutha ngokusobala, ukhiya lonke elinye ithuba ukuze wonke amathuluzi ashintshayo akubuze wena kuqala (ikhadi liyasho ukuthi kungani); Izenzo ezilinganiselayo kuphela embhalweni onobutha ngokusobala futhi amathuluzi weleveli yesistimu kuphela ayeka ukubuza; Isicisha ngokuphelele isihlangu — akukho lutho oluskenwayo, olumisiwe, olumakwayo noma olushintshiwe. Umlayezo omusha ovela kuwe uyawusula.
