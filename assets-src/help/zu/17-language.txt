Izilimi
## Ulimi lohlelo lokusebenza
Izilungiselelo → Ulimi nokuhumusha. Izilimi ezingama-60. Yonke i-interface nemilayezo yethuluzi le-ejenti iyayilandela. Ulimi ngalunye lunefayela lalo lokuhumusha. Noma yimuphi umugqa ifayela lolimi elingakabi nawo uboniswa ngesiNgisi, awulokothi ushiywe ungenalutho.

## Okukhulumayo
I-ejenti ihlale iphendula ngolimi okhuluma nayo, noma yiluphi ulimi emhlabeni. Uma ushintsha, iyashintsha. Isebenzisa indlela yokuhlonipha nenhlonipho ethi "wena" ngolimi lwakho. Amagama ebhrendi nobuchwepheshe ahlala enjengoba enjalo.

## Izilimi zezwi
- Ulimi lokubonwayo (okushoyo kumakrofoni): Izilungiselelo → Izwi nemakrofoni → Imakrofoni nolimi.
- Izwi lokukhuluma: Izilungiselelo → Izwi nemakrofoni → Iphrofayela yezwi, kanye nelebhu Yezwi yezilimi ezifana nesi-Bengali, isi-Hindi, isiNgisi, isi-Urdu, isi-Arabhu, isi-Tamil, isi-Nepali, isi-Spanish, isi-French, isiJalimane, isi-Japanese, isi-Korean, isiShayina, isi-Russian, isi-Turkish.

## Le mibhalo
Sebenzisa ama-chips olimi ngaphezulu kwaleli khasi. Umhlahlandlela wonke ubhalwe ngazo zonke izilimi ezisekelwa uhlelo lokusebenza, ngesandla, futhi zithunyelwe ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza. Akukho okuhunyushwa ocingweni lwakho: sonke isahluko sivuleka khona manjalo, sisebenza ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, futhi ayidingi modeli noma ukhiye.

## Ubhala ngesakho iskripthi
Amadokhumenti, amaphosta nemiklamo kungenziwa kunoma yisiphi isikripthi. Kumiklamo yaseBengali kusetshenziswa ifonti efanele; kwezinye izikripthi ifonti efanayo iyakhethwa.
