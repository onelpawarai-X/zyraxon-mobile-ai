Tips and example requests
## Say what you want, not how
- "Make a landing page for a gym, dark and bold."
- "Build a full-stack booking app with a database." (the agent asks the technical choices first)
- "Open YouTube and play a calm song."
- "Scroll down and open the first result."
- "What is my battery and Wi-Fi?"
- "Read my newest SMS and tell me the code."
- "Call Mom." / "Answer the call."
- "Make a CV from this photo." (attach it)
- "Turn this meeting recording into notes with action items."
- "Make a poster for Friday's event with these colours."
- "Make an audio dialogue between two friends, one boy and one girl."
- "Remember that my sister's birthday is 3 May."
- "Every morning at 8 tell me the weather." (autopilot)
- "Learn how I send my rent by my wallet app so you can do it again." (skill)
- "Undo what you did in the project folder." (timeline)
- "Where is my rental contract?" (brain)
- "Install python packages and run this script." (ZYRAXON OS)
- "Publish this site so I can share it." (public link)

## Habits that help
- Short and clear beats long. The agent chooses defaults and tells you what it chose.
- When it asks one question, answer it and it continues from the same spot.
- Say "explain" or "summary" for a detailed answer; otherwise answers are short.
- You can talk over it at any time. It stops and listens.
- Use Build mode for making things and Agent mode for doing things on the phone.
