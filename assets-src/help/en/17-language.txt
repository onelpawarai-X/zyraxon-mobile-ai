Languages
## App language
Settings → Language & translation. 60 languages. The whole interface and the agent's tool messages follow it. Each language has its own translation file. Any line that a language file does not have yet is shown in English, never left blank.

## What you speak
The agent always answers in the language you speak to it, any language in the world. If you switch, it switches. It uses the friendly-respectful form of "you" in your language. Brand and technology names stay as they are.

## Voice languages
- Recognition language (what you speak into the microphone): Settings → Voice & mic → Mic & language.
- Speaking voice: Settings → Voice & mic → Voice profile, and the Voice lab for languages such as Bengali, Hindi, English, Urdu, Arabic, Tamil, Nepali, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, Turkish.

## This documentation
Use the language chips on top of this page. The whole guide is written out in every language the app supports, by hand, and shipped inside the app. Nothing is translated on your phone: every chapter opens instantly, works offline, and needs no model or key.

## Writing in your own script
Documents, posters and designs can be made in any script. For Bengali designs the right font is used; for other scripts the matching font is chosen.
