Providers, models and keys
## Providers
ZYRAXON (own line and the Apex cloud agent, always connected), OpenCode (free quota), Groq (very fast Llama), Gemini, OpenAI, Grok (xAI), Claude, DeepSeek and OpenRouter. Add any key in Settings → Providers & API. The key is saved on your phone, checked against the provider's catalogue, and the models stream into the selector as each provider answers.

## Choosing a model
Use the model button in the message bar or Settings → Model & identity → Model. Search by name, filter All or Custom. Your choice is saved and restored at launch; a refresh never replaces your saved model. Models marked Live can speak in their own voice (see Live voice).

## On-device models
Settings → Model & identity → On-device. The model runs on the phone with no data leaving it. Files are large (hundreds of MB to a few GB), so download on Wi-Fi. They are listed small to large; on a phone with little RAM choose the lightest. Tool calling is supported by the Gemma and FunctionGemma bundles. A Hugging Face token is only for gated files. Loaded models are remembered.

## The ZYRAXON website
zyraxonai.lovable.app: create an account (email or Google), open Settings, API Key / Access Token, create a key and paste it in the app. Without a key you can still use the app with on-device models and local tools.

## Costs and data
Cloud providers bill you according to their own rules. With "On-device only" turned on nothing is sent to any provider.
